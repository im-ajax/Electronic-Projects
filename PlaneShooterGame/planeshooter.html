ESP32-S3 Pin Mapping (All on 3.3V Rail)

  • Potentiometer (Steering): Center pin → GPIO 1. Outer pins → 3.3V & GND.
  • Touch Sensor (Fire): OUT → GPIO 4. VCC/GND → 3.3V & GND.
  • IR Sensor (Reload): OUT → GPIO 5. VCC/GND → 3.3V & GND.
