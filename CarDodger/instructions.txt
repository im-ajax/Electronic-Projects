================================================================================
          PROJECT BUILD MANUAL: DUAL-IR GESTURE CONTROLLED CAR DODGER
================================================================================

1. HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS & PIN CONFIGURATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMPONENTS:
- 1x Microcontroller (Arduino Uno, Nano, or ESP32)
- 2x Active-Low Infrared Obstacle Sensors (TCRT5000 / LM393 comparator based)
- 1x Solderless Breadboard & Male-to-Male Jumper Wires
- 1x USB Programming & Data Cable

WIRING PINOUT:
- Common Rails:
  * MCU 5V (or 3.3V on ESP32)  -> Breadboard Positive Rail (+)
  * MCU GND                    -> Breadboard Negative Rail (-)

- Left Sensor Module (Steer Left):
  * VCC                        -> Breadboard Positive Rail (+)
  * GND                        -> Breadboard Negative Rail (-)
  * OUT                        -> Arduino Pin D2 (or ESP32 GPIO 18)

- Right Sensor Module (Steer Right):
  * VCC                        -> Breadboard Positive Rail (+)
  * GND                        -> Breadboard Negative Rail (-)
  * OUT                        -> Arduino Pin D3 (or ESP32 GPIO 19)

PHYSICAL PLACEMENT:
Position both sensor modules side by side on your desk, spaced roughly 15 cm 
to 20 cm apart. Angle the emitter/receiver LEDs slightly upward (10 to 15 
degrees) so your left and right hands trigger them independently without 
crossing optical zones.


2. SENSOR CALIBRATION PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Connect the microcontroller to your PC via USB to supply power.
2. Note the two LEDs on each IR module:
   - Power LED: Solid ON.
   - Sensor Output LED: Lights up only when an obstacle is detected.
3. Hover your hand 6 cm to 10 cm directly above the Left Sensor:
   - If the output LED does not turn on: Rotate the onboard blue trimpot 
     CLOCKWISE to increase infrared detection range.
   - If the output LED stays stuck ON (triggered by ceiling lights or desk 
     surfaces): Rotate the trimpot COUNTER-CLOCKWISE until it turns off.
4. Repeat the calibration for the Right Sensor until both sensors register 
   your hands at the exact same height and turn off immediately upon hand 
   removal.


3. FIRMWARE FLASHING & VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the Arduino IDE or PlatformIO.
2. Ensure the firmware uses a 115200 baud rate and includes a 20-25ms debounce 
   filter.
3. Configure sensor pins as INPUT_PULLUP.
4. Confirm the code transmits single-character tokens ('L', 'R', or 'N') ONLY 
   when the stable sensor state changes, keeping the serial buffer empty.
5. Select your target board and COM port, then click Upload.
6. Open the Serial Monitor set to 115200 baud:
   - Wave your left hand: A single 'L' should appear, followed by 'N' when 
     withdrawn.
   - Wave your right hand: A single 'R' should appear, followed by 'N' when 
     withdrawn.
7. CRITICAL: Close the Serial Monitor before opening the browser game. The COM 
   port cannot be shared by two programs simultaneously.


4. BROWSER SETUP & GAME LAUNCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Save the frontend game application as "index.html" on your PC.
2. Open "index.html" using a Chromium-based browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft 
   Edge, Opera, or Brave). Note: Web Serial API is NOT supported on Firefox 
   or Safari.
3. Click the "Connect Arduino" button on the top HUD.
4. From the browser prompt, select your board's serial connection (e.g., 
   USB-SERIAL CH340, CP2102, or Arduino Uno) and click "Connect".
5. The status badge will change from "Hardware Offline" (Red) to "Hardware 
   Connected" (Green).
6. Wave your hands over the sensors to steer your vehicle across lanes. If 
   your car crashes, wave over either sensor to reboot and restart the round.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON MISTAKES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE: Browser displays "Failed to open serial port".
FIX:   Another software (Arduino IDE Serial Monitor, Cura, PuTTY) is currently 
       holding the COM port open. Close all other serial programs and reload 
       the webpage.

ISSUE: Vehicle steers erratically without hand movement.
FIX:   Ambient sunlight or desk bounce is triggering the IR receivers. Reduce 
       sensitivity using the onboard trimpots and slightly elevate the sensor 
       heads away from the desk surface.

ISSUE: Input has a 1-2 second delay.
FIX:   The microcontroller is spamming strings inside void loop() every cycle. 
       Ensure the board transmits data strictly upon state transitions 
       (rawState != lastStableState).

ISSUE: "Connect Arduino" button does nothing.
FIX:   You are opening the page in an unsupported browser (Firefox, Safari) or 
       browser serial permissions are disabled in settings. Use Google Chrome 
       or Microsoft Edge.
